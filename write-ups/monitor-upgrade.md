# Upgrading a workstation monitor setup

**Setting:** Supervised workplace support

## Problem or goal

A workstation needed replacement monitors and a comfortable, usable desk setup.

## What I found

A cubicle with a standing desk in a quiet environment (noise was a concern here). On the desk, the existing monitor setup consisted of two HP Compaq LA2206x monitors on the ends of adjustable arms on a rotatable desk mount, The two cables connected to the monitors were HDMI to DVI cables connected via a dock with multiple HDMI ports so that the user has access via their laptop. For context the user was out of the office.

## My approach

The first step I took was to study the work area and to take notes of the users current setup, for example how they preferred the monitors to be angled, where everything on the desk was positioned so it can be returned to the proper location if it was moved for any reason, then onto documenting the monitor brand and device model, and lastly taking note of the routing path of the HDMI to DVI cable from the dock to the monitor.

The second step I took was to unplug and remove the old HP Compaq LA2206x monitors and have them reattached to the old mounts that were provided by the manufacturer, in order to return them to storage. In this process I also put the new Dell UT419H monitors onto the arms of the users setup and secured both in place. During this process I noticed that the current cables were incompatible with the new monitors.

The third step I took was replacing the old HDMI to DVI cables that had connected the previous monitors, with new HDMI to HDMI cables for the new monitors as they did not have a DVI port. During the replacement I made sure to use existing cable management to the benefit of the user and improved upon it where I could.

The fourth step I took was plugging in all cables; power and HDMI to the monitors and the other end of the HDMI cable to the dock.

The fifth step I took was verifying functionality by checking that the monitors would power on, displayed video and were detected by the workstation.

The sixth step was to replace any objects that were moved around in the user's workspace for access to the monitors, mounts and cabling. This step ensures that the operation was clandestine.

The final step I took was scheduling a follow up meeting to confirm that the user was fully satisfied with the new monitor setup.

During the follow up meeting the user requested a height adjustment of about 1/2 to 3/4 inch.

The instructor over seeing the preceding had also made some adjustments to the Window display options so that they aligned properly with the physical arrangement of the monitors and made a quick font size change in the Microsoft Office 2019 version of outlook for user readability.

## Result

The result was the original work space but new set of monitors, adjusted to the right height with care given for user satisfaction. After completing the replacement, I scheduled a follow up meeting to ensure that the user was fully satisfied with the service provided. A few key takeaways from this ticket would be to keep a users environment intact, to keep volume at an acceptable level and to create follow up meetings with the end user to ensure satisfaction.

## Skills in practice

Peripheral installation; cable management; user follow-up.
