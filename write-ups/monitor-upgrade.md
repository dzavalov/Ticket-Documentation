## Problem or goal

A workstation needed replacement monitors and a comfortable, usable desk setup.

## What I found

[Describe the existing mount and why the display connections needed changing. Include the later height adjustment requested by the user.]

The existing monitor setup consisted of two monitors on the ends of adjustable arms on a rotatable desk mount, The two cables connected to the monitors were HDMI to DVI cables connected via a dock with multiple HDMI ports so that the user has access via their laptop. For context the user was out of the office the day of the monitor upgrade, when the user had returned they needed a change to the monitor arm height.

## My approach

[Explain your monitor replacement, cabling, work-area care, and follow-up. Credit the instructor for the separate Windows display and Outlook adjustments.]
The first step I took was to study the work area and to take notes of the users current setup, for example how they preferred the monitors to be angled, where everything on the desk was positioned so it can be returned to the proper location if it was moved for any reason, then onto documenting the monitor brand and device model, and lastly taking note of the routing path of the HDMI to DVI cable from the dock to the monitor.

The second step I took was to unplug and remove the old monitors and return them to the old mounts that were provided by the manufacturer, for storage. In this process I also put the new monitors onto the arms of the users setup and took note that the current cables were incompatible with the new monitors
